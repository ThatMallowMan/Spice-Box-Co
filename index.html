The Idea

THE SPICE BOX

In an Indian kitchen, the masala box holds the spices a cook trusts most, each in its own compartment, each kept apart so that it stays true. Close the lid and it is one object, ready for any dish.

Spice Box Co. is built the same way. Each compartment is a distinct culinary identity, an ingredient profile or a part of the guest experience. Every one is curated on its own terms. Together they make a single complete evening.

Wellness Hospitality

BEYOND DIET FOOD

Redefining Dining Wellness

Wellness at the table is not a calorie count. It is the feeling of arriving somewhere you can stop thinking. We treat it as a design problem: build trust in the kitchen, remove friction from the room, and engineer an environment where a guest can genuinely relax.

Three principles guide every decision, from how a menu reads to how a chair sits.

Psychological Comfort

Absolute trust in culinary mastery.

When guests trust the kitchen completely, they stop second-guessing the plate and start enjoying it.

Frictionless Experience

Removing all decision fatigue.

Curated sequences, clear guidance and anticipatory service leave guests with one task: to enjoy.

Environmental Rest

Engineering physical tranquility.

Air, sound, light and seating are specified with the same care as the recipes.

The Thai-inspired expressionSIAM
SPICE HOUSE

Siam Spice House is where Thai-inspired flavors, freshness and balance meet the Spice Box philosophy. Herbs are bright, acids are exact, and sweetness, salt and heat sit in measured proportion.

The room is quieter than the rest of the brand: deep jade, curved rooflines, living greenery and a pace that lets every plate settle before the next arrives.

FlavorsLemongrass, galangal, kaffir lime and Thai basil, handled with restraint.
BalanceSour, sweet, salty and hot held in equilibrium on every plate.
CalmTropical greenery, soft light and a deliberately unhurried tempo.
